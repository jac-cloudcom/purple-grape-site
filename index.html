Why we exist

Cannabis is one of the most tightly regulated categories in Europe. Most of the digital tools businesses depend on were never built for it.

Ad platforms restrict it. The rules differ by country and keep changing. General agencies and directories either avoid the category or get it wrong. Purple Grape builds the specialist layer underneath: regulatory information, a partner discovery platform, and digital work for businesses already trading.

How we build

Three rules we don't bend.

  1. 01

    Compliance first

    The rules shape every product from day one. They're never a check bolted on at the end.

  2. 02

    Sourced and dated

    When we publish what a rule says, we show the source and the date it was last checked. That is information, not legal advice.

  3. 03

    Built for the market as it is

    UK and European rules as they stand today, not as anyone wishes they were.
